# Prose review: addendum.md

**Purpose and audience:** I read this addendum as a record for human PRD and architecture readers, chiefly Skander. It holds the client's answers as the PRD cites them, plus the seed rows for the architecture's decision table.

**Style and voice to preserve:** terse, declarative and plain. Bold lead-ins on the bullets. The Expected column is in a clipped, note-like register. British spelling ("authorised") and no serial comma are deliberate. The paraphrase of Danny's email uses the second person ("you can share it") and contractions ("doesn't"). These choices suit Microsoft style, so the review keeps them. "Admin role" is Keycloak's own term and is left alone. Word count: 504 in total, with 164 in the email section.

The structure pass already ran. None of these rows changes the content or the meaning of the paraphrase.
